How the score works

This is not a black-box “ATS score” from a vendor. We extract the text in your browser, then apply a multiplicative model:

  • Keyword match (50%) against the job description you paste (optional).
  • Structure (25%) — standard sections a parser expects.
  • Impact (25%) — metrics and action verbs in bullets.

Template compliance is a multiplier. A multi-column or table-heavy file can hide keywords even if the writing is good. That is why a pretty Canva resume can still score poorly.

The number is a diagnostic, not a job offer. Different employers use different ATS products and settings. We cannot guarantee interviews.
